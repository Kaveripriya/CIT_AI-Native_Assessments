# A2 · AI Systems Essay

CIT AI-Native Engineering Programme · Semester 1

| | |
|---|---|
| **Week** | 6 |
| **Weight** | 15% of Semester 1 grade |
| **Type** | Individual |
| **Length** | 500 words, 5 paragraphs |
| **Submit** | PDF via LMS, before your Week 6 session |

## What you do

Write an essay on one real AI success and one real AI failure. Name the system and the organisation each time. For the failure, explain the root cause, say who was accountable, recommend one change that would have prevented it, and apply a governance framework (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF or similar).

## Files in this folder

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `A2_AI_Systems_Essay_Student_Package.pdf` | Instructions, marking rubric, examples of strong and weak answers, and the essay template |

## How to submit

1. Read the instructions, especially the "Too vague vs Specific enough" table.
2. Pick your two cases. They can be from Weeks 3–5 material or the news.
3. Write all five paragraphs in the template, keeping to 400–600 words in total.
4. Go through the checklist, then upload as **one PDF** on the LMS.
