## What you do

**Part 1, Temperature experiment:** In Google AI Studio, ask the same yes/no question 10 times at temperature 0 and 10 times at temperature 1. Record every response, work out accuracy, unique responses and mode for each, and state the accuracy threshold you would need before deploying.

**Part 2, Cognition case brief:** Analyse the AI failure case assigned to you. Cover what it cost, whether it could have been verified, who was accountable, and which governance control applies.

## Before you start

- Find your **assigned case ID** in your LMS profile.
- Download the one-page case brief from **LMS → A3 Resources**.
- In Google AI Studio, **turn off Grounding with Google Search**. If it's on, your experiment isn't valid.

## How to submit

1. Run the experiment and fill in all 20 rows of the results table, copying each response exactly.
2. Complete the statistics table and the conclusion (100 words max).
3. Complete all six sections of Part 2 for your assigned case.
4. Go through the checklist, then upload both parts as **one PDF** on the LMS.
