# CIT_AI-Native_Assessments


| No. | Project | Domain | What the student builds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mess Feedback Sorter | Food | Labels hostel mess feedback and puts health risks (insects, spoiled food, illness) in an urgent list for the warden |
| 2 | Bus Complaint Sorter | Transport | Labels bus complaints, finds the route, and puts safety complaints in front of the transport officer first |
| 3 | Extension Request Sorter | Education | Reads extension requests, applies the department policy, and sorts each one for faculty to decide |
| 4 | Lab Booking Sorter | Scheduling | Reads free-text lab requests and checks capacity, Sundays and clashes to build a tentative schedule |
| 5 | Clinic Visit Sorter | Health | Sorts campus clinic visit notes by priority, without ever giving a diagnosis |
| 6 | Placement Eligibility Checker | Careers | Reads company criteria and checks which students are eligible, without sending student data to the LLM |
| 7 | Hostel Maintenance Ticket Sorter | Facilities | Sorts hostel repair tickets, checks room numbers, merges duplicates and sets due dates |
| 8 | Club Expense Claim Checker | Finance | Checks club expense claims against budgets and spots one bill split into smaller claims |
| 9 | Lost and Found Matcher | Campus services | Suggests matches between lost and found reports, and flags items two people might claim |
| 10 | Event Registration Cleaner | Events | Cleans messy event registrations, finds duplicates, and lists entries that need fixing |
